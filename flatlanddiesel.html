About Flatland Diesel

Flatland Diesel has been proudly serving Saskatoon and surrounding areas since July 2021. Owned and operated by Simo Toor, a certified Red Seal mechanic, we specialize in heavy-duty truck and trailer repair.

With years of hands-on experience, we are committed to delivering reliable, honest, and high-quality service. Whether it's diagnostics, maintenance, or major repairs, we make sure your equipment stays on the road and working efficiently.

At Flatland Diesel, we believe in hard work, transparency, and building long-term relationships with our customers.
